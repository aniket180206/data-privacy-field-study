Awareness

Protect Your Digital Privacy

Practical habits for safer digital life.

Strong unique passwords

Use different strong passwords for important accounts.

Two-factor authentication

Add a second verification step wherever possible.

Check permissions

Review camera, microphone, contacts and location access.

Spot phishing

Be careful with urgent requests for OTPs, passwords or money.

Verify websites

Check the domain before entering sensitive information.

Limit oversharing

Avoid publishing unnecessary personal information.
