About the Field Study

This study explores how college students understand and practise data privacy in everyday digital life.

Objective

Measure awareness of personal data, privacy policies, permissions, authentication and cybercrime.

Method

A structured 25-question survey is presented through this website and submitted to the project's official Google Form response system.

Academic Context

Viva College · B.Sc IT · Second Year · Division C · Academic Year 2026–27.
