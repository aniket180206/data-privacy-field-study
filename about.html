ABOUT THE FIELD STUDY

Understanding Data Privacy Awareness

A structured academic field study designed to understand the awareness, knowledge and digital safety practices of college students.

01 — Project Background

About This Field Study

The field study titled “Field Study on Awareness of Data Privacy Among College Students” focuses on understanding how students perceive and manage their personal information in the digital environment.

Modern students regularly use smartphones, social media, educational applications, digital payment systems, online shopping platforms and cloud services. These activities can involve the collection, processing, storage and sharing of personal information.

The study therefore examines not only whether students have heard about data privacy, but also whether they follow practical security measures in their everyday digital activities.

03 — Objectives

Objectives of the Study

The project has been designed around practical questions about privacy awareness and digital behaviour.

OBJECTIVE 01

Measure Awareness

Understand how familiar students are with the concept of data privacy and personal information.

OBJECTIVE 02

Study Digital Behaviour

Examine everyday practices involving passwords, social media, permissions and information sharing.

OBJECTIVE 03

Identify Risks

Identify common situations where students may be exposed to privacy or cybersecurity risks.

OBJECTIVE 04

Understand Cybercrime Awareness

Examine awareness and experiences related to online fraud, phishing and other cybercrime.

OBJECTIVE 05

Promote Safe Practices

Encourage practical habits that can help students protect their accounts and personal information.

OBJECTIVE 06

Recommend Improvements

Identify ways colleges can improve privacy and cybersecurity awareness among students.

05 — Methodology

How Data Is Collected

The primary data for this study is collected through a structured questionnaire designed for college students.

The questionnaire consists of multiple-choice and checkbox-based questions so that responses can be organised, compared and represented through charts and statistical summaries.

The survey examines demographic information, privacy knowledge, online behaviour, cybersecurity practices and experiences with online fraud or cybercrime.

Research Scope & Limitation

The study is intended as an academic field project. Its findings represent the responses collected from the selected survey participants and should not automatically be treated as a representation of all college students.
