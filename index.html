01 — Why This Matters

Your Data Is Part of Your Digital Identity

Every day, students interact with websites, mobile applications, social media platforms, payment systems and educational services. Each interaction can involve personal information.

🔐

Personal Information

Names, phone numbers, email addresses, photographs, addresses, identification information and other details can contribute to your digital identity.

📱

Everyday Digital Life

Social media, online shopping, digital payments, gaming, education platforms and cloud services all create digital footprints.

⚠

Real-World Risks

Poor privacy practices can increase exposure to phishing, identity theft, account compromise, financial fraud and social engineering.

02 — About The Study

Understanding Student Awareness

This field study examines the knowledge, attitudes and everyday practices of college students regarding data privacy and online security.

The survey covers privacy awareness, personal information, application permissions, passwords, two-factor authentication, phishing, cybercrime experiences, online payments and safe internet practices.

04 — Research Process

How The Study Works

The project combines awareness material, survey responses, interactive learning and analysis.

01

Learn

Students explore fundamental concepts related to personal data, privacy, cybersecurity and online safety.

02

Respond

Participants complete the field-study questionnaire based on their knowledge, experiences and digital habits.

03

Analyse

Survey responses are reviewed to identify awareness patterns, common risks and areas where additional education can help.
